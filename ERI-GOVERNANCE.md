# Eri Governance Charter 1.0

## Scope

This charter governs administration of Eri License 1.0 and specific written commercial exceptions. It does not transfer copyright ownership and does not revoke rights granted under an existing Eri License version.

## Administrator

Until a separate written instrument assigns the role, Nguyen Ngo is the Administrator. Nguyen Ngo may designate a foundation, trust, corporation, LLC, or successor governance board through a signed, public written instrument. The founder, Nguyen Ngo, has the power to apoint or remove board members any time he wishes or he can simply not do it at all if he does not wish for a board. The Administrator has the ability to revoke licenses, veto proposed future licenses, transfer Administrator authority, approve licenses, suspend an exeption, and change anything he may wish.

## Board

An authorized board consists of at least three voting members. Members are appointed and removed by a two-thirds vote. No person may hold a board position unless the appointment is recorded publicly. The maximum amount of board members allowed at a time is seven members.

## Conflict of Interest

A member with a financial or personal interest in a licensing decision must disclose it before the vote and may not vote on that decision. All disclosures and votes must be recorded publicly.

## Commercial Exceptions

A specific written commercial exception requires approval by at least two-thirds of voting board members and must identify the exact work, licensee, purpose, scope, duration, and terms. Exceptions cannot rewrite Eri License 1.0 or alter rights already granted to compliant recipients. Public exception records must identify the exception's work and scope.

## Future License Versions

A later numbered Eri License may be published only after two-thirds board approval and a signed authorization from the copyright successor or authorized copyright administrator. Historical terms remain available.

No later version may revoke rights already granted under an existing Eri License, apply retroactively to existing copies, or alter an exception previously granted for an existing work.

## Succession

Licensing administration may be transferred only through a signed written instrument defining the successor's authority. Copyright ownership requires the signed transfer instrument required by applicable law.

## Amendments

This charter may be amended only by a two-thirds board vote, with no amendment retroactively reducing recipient rights or removing public exception records.

## Dissolution

If no Administrator or authorized board exists, authority returns to Nguyen Ngo or his lawful copyright successor.
